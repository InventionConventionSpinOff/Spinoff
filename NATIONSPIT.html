How It Works

The science and technology behind Spin Off

Step 1: Kick Detection

An accelerometer and gyroscope detect the exact moment of ball contact, measuring impact force up to 16 G.

Step 2: Data Collection

Sensors continuously sample at high frequency, capturing acceleration on all three axes plus rotational velocity.

Step 3: Signal Processing

A low-pass filter removes noise while preserving critical data. Peak detection identifies maximum speed and spin.

Step 4: Wireless Transmission

Bluetooth Low Energy streams a JSON data packet to your device in real time after every kick.

Step 5: AI Analysis

The app and AI analyze your technique, track trends, and generate personalized coaching advice.

How the App Works

Spin Off captures ball sensor data for every kick and stores each session locally in your browser. The app turns raw speed, spin and impact readings into easy-to-read analytics, lets you export CSV or JSON reports, and saves your progress so you can compare training sessions over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I save my session?
The app saves sessions locally when you start a new session or export data. View saved history on the Live Tracker page.

Q: What does spin axis mean?
The device classifies rotation as topspin, backspin, sidespin or mixed based on which gyroscope axis dominates.

Q: How do exports and sharing work?
Use Export JSON / CSV to download a report. Share Report copies the summary to the clipboard or opens the browser share dialog.

Q: Which browser works best?
Chrome, Edge and Opera support Web Bluetooth and give the most reliable connection.

Physics Behind the Metrics

Speed: Estimated from peak impact acceleration via an impulse model.

Spin Rate: Gyroscope angular velocity converted from degrees/second to RPM.

Launch Angle: Derived from the tilt of the impact acceleration vector.

Flight Time: Estimated using projectile-motion equations.

Sensor Technology

LSM6DS3: 6-axis IMU with ±16 g acceleration and ±2000°/s gyroscope range.

PDM Microphone: Analyzes the impact sound to grade strike quality.

Noise Reduction: Digital low-pass filter (alpha 0.6) removes vibration artifacts.

Power Efficiency: Smart wake/sleep modes extend battery life.

Technology Stack

Built with cutting-edge hardware and software

Hardware

Microcontroller: Seeed XIAO nRF52840 Sense
Processor: Nordic nRF52840 (64 MHz ARM Cortex-M4)
IMU: LSM6DS3TR-C 6-axis accelerometer + gyroscope
Microphone: Built-in PDM microphone
Wireless: Bluetooth 5.0 Low Energy
Power: 1000mAh LiPo battery (JST 1.25mm)
Weight: Regulation soccer ball compatible

Software

Firmware: C++ (Arduino framework, mbed-enabled core)
Web App: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
Charts: Chart.js for real-time data visualization
AI Engine: AMIR AI — rule-based + machine-learning coaching
Connectivity: Web Bluetooth API
Data Format: JSON streamed over a single BLE characteristic
Browser Support: Chrome, Edge, Opera (Desktop & Mobile)

Our Mission

We created Spin Off because professional-level sports analytics shouldn't be limited to elite athletes with expensive equipment. Every player deserves data-driven insights to improve technique, track progress, and reach their goals. By combining affordable hardware with powerful AI, we're making sports analytics accessible to players of all levels.

The Journey

October 2025: Initial concept — "What if we could track soccer ball data like professional teams?"

November 2025: Research phase — choosing the best parts for the build.

December 2025: Prototype 1 — basic Arduino setup with accelerometer readings.

January 2026: Final version — added a gyroscope, microphone, web app and AI coaching.
